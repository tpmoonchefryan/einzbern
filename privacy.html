dot-drive-sync

Privacy policy

Effective upon publication. Prepared on 17 November 2026.

This policy describes the owner's personal rclone-based Google Drive synchronization configuration, called dot-drive-sync. It does not describe unrelated software in the repository hosting these pages.

Google data accessed and purpose

The application accesses file and folder contents, names, identifiers, timestamps, sizes, checksums, and other metadata needed for synchronization. It may read basic account information to confirm that the intended Google account is connected.

The application requests Google Drive read and write access to synchronize the folder selected by the owner. Synchronization can create, modify, move, or delete files on both sides. The configured folder is an operational limit, not a restriction on the OAuth grant.

Storage and transfer

File data is transferred between Google Drive and the owner's computer. Synchronized local copies, synchronization listings, diagnostic logs, and recovery backups are stored on the owner's devices. Logs and listings may contain file names and metadata.

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored separately from the synchronized folder with access restricted to the service account and the device administrator. They allow scheduled synchronization without signing in for each run. This website does not receive these credentials or file data.

Use and sharing

The synchronization process uses Google data to copy and reconcile files selected by the owner and to provide recovery copies. It does not transmit those files to an advertising, analytics, or machine-learning service. Other programs that the owner separately runs on synchronized files are outside this synchronization process. The selected files may remain available to their existing collaborators under their Google Drive permissions.

Retention and removal

Synchronized copies are retained until the owner changes or deletes them. Local recovery backups are retained until the owner removes them; they do not automatically expire in this deployment. Diagnostic logs follow the device's log-retention settings.

The owner can stop scheduled synchronization, remove local copies and backups, and revoke dot-drive-sync in Google Account connections. Revoking access stops future authorized access; it does not delete copies already stored on the owner's devices or in Google Drive.

Website hosting

These static informational pages will be hosted on GitHub Pages. They contain no sign-in form, analytics script, or advertising. GitHub may process visitor information, including IP addresses, to operate and secure its hosting service. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Contact and updates

The operator is the account owner identified by the support email on the Google OAuth consent screen. Use that support address for questions about this deployment. This policy will be updated before the application adopts a materially different data-handling practice.
